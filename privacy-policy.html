Summary

Linda Chirwa Attorneys collects personal information in order to provide legal services, respond to enquiries, and comply with our legal and regulatory obligations. We treat all client information as confidential and protected by attorney–client privilege. We do not sell your personal information. You have the right to access, correct, or delete your information, and to object to certain uses.

1. Who we are

Linda Chirwa Attorneys ("the firm", "we", "us", or "our") is a firm of attorneys, conveyancers, and notaries registered with the Legal Practice Council of South Africa. Our registration number is 2024/593757/21.

Our head office is at Block C, Stoneridge Office Park, Greenstone, Modderfontein, Johannesburg, Gauteng, 1609.

For the purposes of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA"), we are the responsible party for personal information processed through this website and in the course of providing legal services.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to personal information we collect through:

  • This website and any subdomains;
  • Contact forms, enquiry forms, and correspondence submitted through the site;
  • Emails, phone calls, and WhatsApp messages you send to us;
  • Any services we provide to you as a client or prospective client.

This policy does not apply to third-party websites linked from our site, or to information collected by third parties (such as Google, Meta, or our hosting provider) under their own privacy policies.

3. What we collect

The personal information we collect depends on how you interact with us. It may include:

Information you give us

  • Identity information: name, surname, identity number, passport number, date of birth, gender, marital status.
  • Contact information: email address, phone number, physical address, WhatsApp number.
  • Matter information: details of your legal matter, the counterparties involved, and any documentation you provide.
  • Financial information: bank account details, income information, and payment details, where required to provide legal services.
  • Communication content: the content of any messages, emails, or form submissions you send to us.
  • Special categories of personal information: where relevant to your matter, we may process information about your health, criminal record, or other sensitive categories. We do so only where necessary and where permitted by law.

Information we collect automatically

  • Usage data: pages visited, time on site, referring URLs, and general interaction data (via analytics cookies).
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system.

Information we receive from third parties

  • Information provided by other attorneys, counsel, sheriffs, the Master of the High Court, or the Deeds Office;
  • Information obtained through public registers or searches required to provide legal services;
  • Information provided by your bank, employer, or financial advisor where you have authorised them to share it.

4. How we collect it

We collect personal information:

  • Directly from you when you fill in a form on our site, call us, WhatsApp us, or email us;
  • When you become a client and provide documentation as part of your matter;
  • Automatically through cookies and similar technologies when you use our site (see our Cookie Policy);
  • From third parties as described above.

5. Why we use it

We process personal information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry. We use the contact information you provide to reply to your message and assess whether we can assist you.
  • To provide legal services. Once you instruct us, we use your information to represent you, draft documents, engage with third parties, and manage your matter.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations. This includes FICA, anti-money laundering, tax, and Legal Practice Council requirements.
  • To manage and improve our website. To understand how visitors use the site and to keep it secure.
  • To send you information you have asked for. If you have consented, we may send you legal updates or firm news. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To protect our legal rights. Where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

6. Legal basis for processing

Under POPIA, we may only process your personal information if there is a lawful basis. We rely on the following:

  • Your consent: where you have given it (for example, marketing communications);
  • Performance of a contract: to provide legal services you have engaged us to perform;
  • Compliance with a legal obligation: such as FICA and anti-money laundering obligations;
  • Legitimate interests: such as responding to your enquiry and protecting our firm's legal rights;
  • Vital interests: where processing is necessary to protect your life or that of another person;
  • Public interest: where processing is necessary for the proper administration of justice.

7. Who we share it with

We treat all client information as confidential. However, in the course of providing legal services, we may share your information with:

  • Legal professionals: counsel, correspondents, experts, and notaries involved in your matter;
  • Courts and regulators: the courts, the Master of the High Court, the Deeds Office, the Legal Practice Council, and FICA compliance authorities;
  • Service providers: our IT, hosting, email, and document management providers, who process information on our behalf under confidentiality obligations;
  • Financial institutions: banks and other institutions where required to complete a transaction on your instructions;
  • Counterparties: where necessary in the course of your matter;
  • Professional advisors: our own accountants, auditors, and insurers;
  • Legal successors: in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of the firm.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

8. How long we keep it

We retain personal information for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, and to comply with our legal, regulatory, and professional obligations. In practice:

Type of information Retention period
Enquiries that do not become client matters 12 months from last contact
Client matter files Minimum 7 years after the matter is closed, or longer where required by law
FICA records Minimum 5 years after the end of the business relationship
Conveyancing records As required by the Legal Practice Council and Deeds Office regulations
Website analytics data 26 months (Google Analytics default retention)

9. How we protect it

We take the security of your personal information seriously. Safeguards we have in place include:

  • Encrypted transmission of data over HTTPS;
  • Access controls limiting who within the firm can view your information;
  • Secure storage of physical and electronic records;
  • Confidentiality obligations on all staff and service providers;
  • Regular review of our information security practices.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no system is completely secure. If you have reason to believe your information has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

10. Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the following rights in relation to your personal information:

  • Right to be notified: to know when your information is being collected;
  • Right to access: to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • Right to correction: to have inaccurate, misleading, or outdated information corrected or deleted;
  • Right to deletion: to request deletion of personal information that we are no longer entitled to retain;
  • Right to object: to object to processing based on legitimate interests, or to processing for direct marketing;
  • Right to restrict processing: in certain circumstances, to limit how we use your information;
  • Right to data portability: where technically feasible, to receive your information in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • Right to withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent, to withdraw it at any time;
  • Right to lodge a complaint: with the Information Regulator (see below).

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Information Officer using the details at the bottom of this page. We will respond within 30 days, as required by POPIA.

11. Cross-border transfers

Some of the third-party service providers we use (such as Google for analytics and email, and Facebook for video hosting) may store or process data outside South Africa. Where we transfer personal information outside South Africa, we do so only where:

  • The recipient is subject to laws providing an adequate level of protection for personal information;
  • The recipient is bound by a binding agreement requiring it to provide protection substantially similar to POPIA;
  • You have consented to the transfer; or
  • The transfer is necessary for the performance of a contract with you, or for the conclusion or performance of a contract concluded in your interest.

12. Children

Our services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 18 without the consent of a competent person. Where a minor is involved in a legal matter, their information is processed only as required to provide legal services, and under the supervision of a parent or guardian.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in applicable law. The updated version will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date at the top will be amended. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

14. Complaints

If you believe we have processed your personal information unlawfully, you have the right to complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa:

  • Website: inforegulator.org.za
  • Email: complaints.IR@justice.gov.za
  • Phone: 010 023 5200
  • Address: JD House, 27 Stiemens Street, Braamfontein, Johannesburg, 2001

We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concern directly before you approach the Regulator. Please contact our Information Officer first.
