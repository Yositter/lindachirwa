What We Handle

Contract Drafting

We draft agreements from your instructions, not from a generic template, and we make sure the terms suit how your business really works.

Agreements we draft

  • Sale, supply and distribution agreements
  • Service, consulting and subcontracting agreements
  • Agency and reseller agreements
  • Loan agreements and acknowledgements of debt
  • Confidentiality and non-disclosure agreements
  • Standard terms and conditions for customers and suppliers
Review & Negotiation

Before you sign, we read the agreement the way the other side's lawyer will read it in a dispute.

What we check

  • Payment, delivery and acceptance terms
  • Termination, cancellation and breach clauses
  • Limits on liability and indemnities
  • Jurisdiction, notices and the address chosen for legal service
  • Whole-agreement and non-variation clauses, which can stop later informal changes from counting
  • Obligations that are unclear, one-sided or impossible to perform

We tell you plainly which points are worth fighting for and which are not.

Commercial Leases

Lease terms decide your costs and your flexibility for years.

What we handle

  • Drafting and negotiating commercial lease agreements for landlords and tenants
  • Escalation, renewal, deposit and repair clauses
  • Long leases that must be registered by notarial deed, through our Notarial Services
  • Cancellation, arrears and eviction, through our civil litigation team
Suretyships & Security

A suretyship makes someone personally liable for another's debt. The law requires it to be in writing and signed by the surety, and the wording matters.

What we handle

  • Drafting and reviewing suretyships, guarantees and cession of debts
  • Advising directors before they sign personal suretyships
  • Notarial bonds over movable assets, with our notaries
  • Enforcing and defending claims on suretyships
Consumer, Credit & Online Terms

Contracts with consumers must comply with the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 and, for credit, the National Credit Act 34 of 2005.

What we handle

  • Consumer-facing terms and conditions
  • Compliance with rules on unfair terms, fixed-term contracts and cancellation
  • Credit agreements and credit provider compliance
  • Website terms, online sales terms and electronic contracting
  • POPIA clauses and data processing agreements
Franchise & Licensing

Franchise agreements and licences are regulated, and they combine contract law with intellectual property.

What we handle

  • Franchise agreements for franchisors and franchisees
  • Disclosure documents and compliance with the franchise rules
  • Licence and distribution agreements
  • Protecting the brand through our IP team

How We Work

  1. Understand the deal We ask what you have agreed, what matters most to you and what could go wrong.
  2. Draft or review We prepare the agreement, or mark up the one you have been sent, with a short summary of the real risks.
  3. Negotiate We deal with the other side, or advise you while you do, and we keep the document consistent as terms change.
  4. Sign properly We make sure the right people sign in the right capacity, with the right formalities.
  5. Keep a record You receive the signed agreement and a note of key dates, such as renewals, review dates and notice periods.

What to Bring to Your First Consultation

Having these ready lets us give you a faster, more useful assessment. We will confirm exactly what is needed once we understand your situation.

  • The draft agreement, or the key terms you have agreed
  • Full names and registration details of every party
  • Any emails or quotations that record what was agreed
  • The commercial terms: price, payment dates, delivery, duration
  • Any previous agreements with the same party
  • Your concerns, and the clauses you are unsure about
  • The date by which the contract must be signed
  • Who will sign for each party, and in what capacity

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a verbal agreement binding?

Often yes, but it is hard to prove, and some contracts must be in writing to be valid, such as the sale of land and suretyships. A written agreement avoids arguments about what was said.

Can I use a template from the internet?

You can, but templates rarely fit your business and may not comply with South African law. A short review by an attorney usually costs far less than the dispute a bad template can cause.

What if the other party breaches the contract?

It depends on the clauses and the breach. You may be able to demand performance, cancel and claim damages. Do not cancel before getting advice, because wrongful cancellation can itself be a breach. Our litigation team can help.

Does a contract have to be signed by a lawyer or notary?

No. Most contracts need only the parties' signatures. A few, such as long leases and antenuptial contracts, need a notary.

How long does a contract review take?

A simple agreement can be reviewed in a few days. Larger or negotiated agreements take longer. We agree a timeline with you at the start.
