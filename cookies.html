Summary

This website sets one cookie of its own, to remember your cookie choice. Maps and videos on some pages come from Google and Facebook, and we load them only if you allow third-party content. We do not sell your data, and we do not use cookies to build advertising profiles of you. You can change your choice at any time from the Cookie Settings link in the footer, or through your browser settings.

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to provide information to the owners of the site. Cookies do not contain programs and cannot carry viruses.

Similar technologies include web beacons, pixel tags, and local storage. Where this policy refers to "cookies," it includes these similar technologies.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for two reasons:

  • To remember your choice. One first-party cookie records whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies, so we do not ask you again on every visit.
  • To show content from other providers, only with your permission. The map on our contact page and the videos on our resources page are hosted by Google and Facebook. These providers may set their own cookies, so the content stays blocked until you allow it.

If we switch on website analytics, it will load only after you accept analytics cookies. We do not use cookies for behavioural advertising, retargeting, or to build personal profiles of our visitors.

3. Types of cookies we use

Cookies set by this website

The only cookie we set ourselves is the one that stores your consent choice. It is strictly necessary for the banner to work, so it is always on. It holds your choices and the date you made them, and nothing that identifies you.

Cookie name Purpose Duration Category
cookie_consent Stores your cookie choices so we do not ask you again on every visit. 12 months Strictly necessary

Analytics cookies (only if switched on)

If we enable Google Analytics, the cookies below are set only after you accept analytics cookies, and they are removed if you later withdraw consent.

Cookie name Purpose Duration Category
_ga Google Analytics: distinguishes unique users by assigning a randomly generated number as a client identifier. 2 years Analytics
_ga_* Google Analytics 4: used to persist session state. 2 years Analytics
_gid Google Analytics: distinguishes users for a 24-hour period. 24 hours Analytics

Note: this table will be updated if additional cookies are added to the website. Third-party cookie names, purposes, and durations are set by the third parties themselves and are outside our control.

4. Third-party cookies

Some pages include content from third-party services. This content is blocked until you choose "Accept all" or switch on "Third-party content" in your cookie preferences. Until then, nothing is requested from these providers. We use the following services:

Google Analytics (if switched on)

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site, which pages are most popular, how visitors navigate, and where they come from. Google Analytics collects information anonymously and reports website trends without identifying individual visitors. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google Maps

Our contact page embeds a Google Map to show the location of our offices. Google may set cookies when the map is loaded, which happens only after you allow third-party content. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Facebook (video embeds)

Our resources page embeds videos hosted on Facebook. Facebook may set cookies when the video player is loaded, which happens only after you allow third-party content. Please refer to Meta's Privacy Policy for more information.

Fonts

The typefaces on this site are served from our own web server. No font requests are sent to Google or any other provider, and no cookies are set to display them.

5. Managing cookies

You can change your choice at any time. Open cookie preferences, or use the Cookie Settings link in the footer of any page.

You have the right to accept or reject cookies. You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our website, though some functionality and areas of the site may be restricted.

The means by which you can refuse cookies through your browser depend on your browser. Here are links to instructions for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Do Not Track

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature. Because there is currently no industry-wide standard for how to interpret Do Not Track signals, our website does not currently respond to them. Where required by applicable law, we will update this policy.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date at the top will be amended. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
