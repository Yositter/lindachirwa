Summary

This website uses a small number of cookies to make the site work properly and to understand how visitors use it. We do not sell your data, and we do not use cookies to build advertising profiles of you. You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to provide information to the owners of the site. Cookies do not contain programs and cannot carry viruses.

Similar technologies include web beacons, pixel tags, and local storage. Where this policy refers to "cookies," it includes these similar technologies.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for three reasons:

  • To make the website work. Some features, such as the mobile navigation, form submission security, and remembering your cookie preferences, require cookies to function.
  • To understand how the site is used. We use analytics cookies to see which pages are visited, how long visitors stay, and where they come from. This helps us improve the site.
  • To protect the site and our visitors. Security cookies help us detect and prevent fraudulent form submissions and abuse.

We do not use cookies for behavioural advertising, retargeting, or to build personal profiles of our visitors.

3. Types of cookies we use

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They are usually set in response to actions you take, such as setting your privacy preferences or submitting a form. They do not store any personally identifiable information.

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies on this site are set by third-party services that appear on our pages. We use the following third-party services:

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site, which pages are most popular, how visitors navigate, and where they come from. Google Analytics collects information anonymously and reports website trends without identifying individual visitors. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google Maps

Our contact page embeds a Google Map to show the location of our offices. Google may set cookies when the map is loaded. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Facebook (video embeds)

Our resources page embeds videos hosted on Facebook. Facebook may set cookies when the video player is loaded. Please refer to Meta's Privacy Policy for more information.

5. Managing cookies

You have the right to accept or reject cookies. You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our website, though some functionality and areas of the site may be restricted.

The means by which you can refuse cookies through your browser depend on your browser. Here are links to instructions for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Do Not Track

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature. Because there is currently no industry-wide standard for how to interpret Do Not Track signals, our website does not currently respond to them. Where required by applicable law, we will update this policy.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date at the top will be amended. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
